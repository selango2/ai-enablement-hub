# Perficient AI Adoption Operating Model
## Sales Pitch Deck (6 Slides)

**Duration:** 10 minutes  
**Audience:** Enterprise prospects (CIO, VP Digital, Chief AI Officer)  
**Goal:** Discovery → Schedule implementation conversation  

---

## SLIDE 1: Opening (1 minute)

**Title:** "Shadow AI Is Costing You Millions"

**Visual:** Two paths (fast/slow) with $ symbols

**Talking Points:**
- "How many of your teams are using ChatGPT or other AI tools without going through your official process?"
- "Most companies we talk to say 60-70% of knowledge workers are using unsanctioned AI"
- "Here's why: your sanctioned path is slower than the unsanctioned one"

**Discovery Questions:**
- "Are you seeing shadow AI in your organization?"
- "What's your current approval process for new AI tools?"
- "How long does it take from request to tool in hand?"

**Speaker Notes:** Lead with recognition ("You probably see this too"). Don't blame them. Make them nod.

---

## SLIDE 2: The Real Cost (1 minute)

**Title:** "What Does Shadow AI Cost You?"

**Visual:** Cost breakdown chart
```
Direct costs:
- Duplicate tool purchases (multiple teams buying same tool)
- Untracked licensing and API spend
- Unvetted vendors (potential security/compliance exposure)

Indirect costs:
- Governance time (chasing down unsanctioned usage)
- Compliance risk (data exposure, audit issues)
- Lost productivity (time spent finding/evaluating tools vs. using approved ones)
- Duplicated work (teams solving same problems independently)

Total for typical 500-person organization:
→ $1.5-3M annually
```

**Talking Points:**
- "Most companies don't track this, so they don't see it"
- "But it's real money—and it's growing"
- "We can help you quantify it"

**Discovery Question:**
- "If I could show you how much shadow AI is costing you, would that change your budgeting?"

**Speaker Notes:** Make this concrete. Give examples from their industry if you have them.

---

## SLIDE 3: The Problem With Traditional Governance (1 minute)

**Title:** "Why Adding More Gates Doesn't Work"

**Visual:** Venn diagram or comparison table

**Most Companies Try:**
- ❌ Stricter policies ("No unsanctioned tools")
- ❌ More approvals ("Get CIO sign-off")
- ❌ More controls ("Audit everything")

**What Happens:**
- Teams feel constrained
- Shadow AI grows (not shrinks)
- Governance looks like obstruction
- Adoption stalls at pilot

**Why It Fails:**
- "You can't outrun shadow AI with policies"
- "Users will always choose the fastest path"
- "Governance becomes the bottleneck, not the enabler"

**The Insight:**
> "The fastest sanctioned path beats the unsanctioned one"

**Talking Points:**
- "Traditional governance is reactive"
- "We recommend a different approach"
- "One that makes the sanctioned path faster"

**Speaker Notes:** Validate their pain without judgment.

---

## SLIDE 4: The Perficient Approach (2 minutes)

**Title:** "Hub-and-Spoke Operating Model"

**Visual:** Simple diagram or flowchart

**Three Key Elements:**

**1. Two-Track Intake**
```
Track A (Low-risk)        Track B (High-risk)
Approved tools      →     Requires review
Approved use cases        Client data involved
No approval gate          Formal intake
Timeline: Days            Timeline: 48-72 hours
```
*Both move fast. One doesn't need a gate.*

**2. Champions Program**
- Influential leaders in each business unit
- Enable their teams → don't manage them
- Leverage (not headcount)

**3. Measurement System**
- Per-user budgets + alerts
- Baseline before → outcome after
- Quantify impact (time saved, cost reduction, quality improvement)

**Talking Points:**
- "This isn't about being stricter—it's about being smarter"
- "We make the sanctioned path so good that teams prefer it"
- "And we measure what matters: adoption + impact"

**Discovery Questions:**
- "How do you currently handle AI tool requests?"
- "Who are your natural champions (influential leaders)?"
- "What would success look like for you?"

**Speaker Notes:** Paint a picture of a better way. Make them imagine their world with this in place.

## SALES GUIDE: DISCOVERY CONVERSATION

### Pre-Call Research
- Company size (employees, annual revenue)
- Current AI tool usage (if public)
- Any governance initiatives they've mentioned
- Recent C-suite changes (CIO, CISO, Chief Digital Officer)

### Opening (2 min)
- "I've been talking to [industry] companies about how they're scaling AI safely"
- "A pattern we're seeing: shadow AI is the default, governance is too slow"
- "I thought it might be worth a conversation to see if we're seeing the same thing"

### Discovery Questions (5-7 min)
1. "How is your organization approaching AI adoption right now?"
2. "What tools are your teams using officially vs. unofficially?"
3. "How long does it take from request to approval for a new tool?"
4. "What's your biggest friction point in the process?"
5. "Who owns AI adoption / governance in your organization?"
6. "If you could fix one thing, what would it be?"
7. "What would success look like for you in the next 6 months?"

### Problem Validation (3-5 min)
- Listen for: slow approvals, duplicate tools, compliance concerns, adoption stalling
- Reflect back: "So you're seeing shadow AI, and your governance process is slowing things down"
- Validate: "This is exactly what we're hearing across [industry/similar companies]"

### Introduction to Perficient Approach (3-5 min)
- "We've built a framework specifically for this situation"
- "It's not about more gates—it's about making the right path faster"
- "We call it the Hub-and-Spoke Operating Model"
- Show: SLIDE 4

### Exploring Fit (3-5 min)
- "Do you think a 90-day pilot makes sense?"
- "Who would we need to involve in an initial conversation?"
- "What would we need to show you to move forward?"

### Closing (2 min)
- If interested: "Let's schedule a workshop to scope this specific to your organization"
- If uncertain: "Can I send you some more info and we can reconnect in a week?"
- If not interested: "I appreciate your time. If things change, feel free to reach out"

### Post-Call Sequence
- Send: SERVICE_DESCRIPTION.md + ROI calculator
- Note: Who they said needed to be involved
- Task: Set follow-up in 1 week

## OBJECTION HANDLING

**"We don't have a shadow AI problem"**
- Response: "Most teams don't think they do until we look. Would you be open to a quick audit to see what's actually being used?"
- Follow: Offer discovery call to assess

**"We already have governance in place"**
- Response: "Great. How fast is your approval process? And what percentage of your teams are using unsanctioned tools?"
- Follow: If slow or high shadow adoption, you have a fit

**"This sounds expensive"**
- Response: "What would you estimate shadow AI costs you annually? [Let them guess]. Most organizations find it's $1-3M. Our service costs $300K and pays for itself in 2-3 months."
- Follow: Share ROI calculator

**"We need to think about it"**
- Response: "Absolutely. What specific questions should I answer to help you decide?"
- Follow: Send materials, set 1-week follow-up call

**"We're not ready for this yet"**
- Response: "I understand. Can I check back in [3-6 months] to see if the timing is better?"
- Follow: Add to CRM, periodic touchpoints

**"Can you do this as part of a larger engagement?"**
- Response: "Absolutely. In fact, many customers find this is the foundation for successful AI transformation. Want to talk about how it fits with your broader strategy?"
- Follow: Position as Tier 1 in larger roadmap

## SUCCESS METRICS FOR SALES

**For a successful pitch deck:**
- ✅ Prospect nods at "shadow AI problem" (validation)
- ✅ Prospect asks "how much does this cost?" (interest)
- ✅ Prospect says "we could see value in this" (buy-in)
- ✅ Prospect names who needs to be involved (path to decision)
- ✅ You schedule next meeting (progress)

**For a successful discovery call:**
- ✅ You understand their shadow AI situation
- ✅ You've identified 3-5 pain points
- ✅ They see the connection to your framework
- ✅ You know who the buyer is
- ✅ You have a clear next step (workshop, proposal, etc.)

**For a successful proposal:**
- ✅ Customized to their situation (not generic)
- ✅ Shows specific ROI (based on their size/situation)
- ✅ Clear scope (what's included in 90 days)
- ✅ Clear timeline (when they start, when they see results)
- ✅ Clear next step (kickoff date if approved)
