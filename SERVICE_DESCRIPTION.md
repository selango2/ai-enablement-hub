# Perficient AI Adoption Operating Model
## Service Description (1-Page Handout)

---

## THE PROBLEM

**70% of enterprise knowledge workers are using unsanctioned AI tools.**

Why? Because the sanctioned path is slower than the unsanctioned one.

Research analyst needs to analyze competitive intelligence:
- **Sanctioned:** 5-7 day approval process → analysis in week 2-3
- **Unsanctioned:** Open ChatGPT → analysis in 5 minutes

**Result:** Shadow AI becomes the default. Governance becomes a bottleneck. Adoption stalls at pilot stage.

---

## THE SOLUTION

**Hub-and-Spoke Operating Model**

Central hub sets standards, runs governance, measures impact.
Business units execute with approved tools through champions.

### Key Components

**Two-Track Intake**
- **Track A (Low-risk):** Approved tools, approved use cases → Days
- **Track B (High-risk):** Client data, confidential material → 48-72 hour SLAs

**Training-as-Gate**
- 7 personas × 4-session training arcs
- Tool access unlocked through role-specific training
- Behavior change, not just seat licenses

**Per-User Budgets**
- Monthly budget + daily alerts = personal accountability
- Makes AI cost salient, prevents runaway usage

**Governance in System Prompts**
- Encode rules directly into the AI
- Foundational governance, not bolt-on review

### Results

- **Adoption:** 70-90% of target users (vs. 20-30% in pilot-only stage)
- **Time-to-value:** 90 days to operational governance (not ongoing consulting)
- **Speed:** Sanctioned path 5x faster than old process
- **Compliance:** Transparent, auditable, measurable

## ENGAGEMENT STRUCTURE

### 90-Day Implementation

**Phase 1 (Days 1-30):** Governance Setup
- Establish governance council
- Evaluate tool landscape
- Codify policies (4-page operating document)
- Identify champions (6-9 per company)

**Phase 2 (Days 31-60):** Pilot Launch
- 2-3 pilots running with low-risk workflow
- Training tracks launched with early adopters
- Budget system deployed with visibility
- Early wins documented and socialized

**Phase 3 (Days 61-90):** Measure & Scale
- Results quantified against baselines
- Business case updated with actual ROI
- Enterprise roadmap finalized
- Go-to-market and scale plan approved

### Deliverables

✓ Operating policy (4 pages, clear + actionable)
✓ Tool evaluation matrix (technology stack recommendations)
✓ Champions program playbook (how to activate and support)
✓ Training curriculum (7 personas, 28 sessions)
✓ Budget and measurement dashboard (setup + training)
✓ 90-day results report (with ROI projection)
